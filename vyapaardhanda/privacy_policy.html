Vyapaar Dhanda - Official Privacy Policy

Your privacy, business confidentiality, and data sovereignty are our highest priorities.

100% Offline Primary Operation: Core business records—including customer Khata ledgers, stock inventory, sales invoices, supplier accounts, and staff payroll—are stored locally on your smartphone in an encrypted SQLite Room database.

1. On-Device & Cloud Data Storage

  • Local Primary Storage: All core business records are saved on your smartphone in private local app storage.
  • Cloud Backup & Multi-Device Sync: Optional cloud storage enables multi-device synchronization and prevents total data loss in case of phone theft, liquid damage, or device replacement.
  • Cloud Security Measures: Cloud data transmissions are protected using TLS/HTTPS protocols and stored with AES-256 bit encryption. Access is strictly restricted to your authenticated business account.

2. Strict Zero Data Sharing Policy

  • We DO NOT sell, rent, license, trade, or monetize your customer phone numbers, sales figures, credit ledgers, or business transactions to third-party ad networks, brokers, or marketing agencies.
  • Data Sharing Exceptions: Data is shared only when strictly required by law (legal mandates) or when you explicitly initiate an external action (such as sharing an invoice PDF or payment reminder via WhatsApp/SMS).

3. Voice Data & Third-Party AI Processing

  • Voice Input: Audio for voice commands is processed strictly during active microphone recording turns using the standard Android Speech Recognition framework.
  • Google Gemini AI Integration: AI Assistant queries are processed via secure Google Gemini REST API endpoints over encrypted HTTPS. Queries are anonymized without attaching your personal identity or shop name.

4. Diagnostic Data, Crash Reports & Performance Logs

To maintain high reliability, fix software bugs, and optimize performance across diverse Android devices, our system automatically collects non-identifiable technical diagnostics, stack trace crash logs, and hardware performance metrics.

5. Permissions We Request & Operational Purpose

  • 🎤 Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Needed for hands-free AI Voice Assistant commands (billing, stock queries in Hindi/English).
  • 📷 Camera (CAMERA): Needed for quick barcode scanning during counter sales, QR code scanning, and store logo uploads.
  • 📇 Contacts (READ_CONTACTS): Needed to easily select customer and supplier contact numbers when creating Khata entries.
  • 🔔 Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Needed to alert you about pending Udhar payment dues, low stock warnings, and backup reminders.
  • 🌐 Internet (INTERNET): Needed for Google Gemini AI processing, payment QR verification, and optional cloud backup sync.

6. User Rights & Data Choices

  • Right to Export & Backup: You have complete freedom to generate and export full offline JSON data backups to your phone storage anytime in Settings.
  • Right to Delete & Revoke: You can clear local application database records, delete cloud backups, or revoke hardware permissions directly via Android System Settings at any time.

7. Children's Privacy Protection

  • Age Restriction: Vyapaar Dhanda is a professional business billing, inventory, and accounting application designed strictly for business owners, merchants, and adults (aged 18 or older).
  • No Collection from Minors: We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal data or business information from children under the age of 13 (or under 18). If you suspect a minor has provided data, please contact us immediately to have it erased.

8. Robust Security Practices & Technical Safeguards

  • Encryption Standards: All cloud data transfers use HTTPS/TLS 1.3 secure network protocol. Stored cloud backups are guarded with AES-256 bit military-grade encryption.
  • On-Device Application Sandbox: Local database records are stored in private Android app storage inaccessible to other apps on your device.
  • API Key & Credential Hygiene: Secrets and keys for AI processing are secured via runtime build config layers without exposing user credentials.
  • Data Integrity & Local Backups: Automated integrity checks and offline JSON export functionality allow complete user-managed data recovery.
