Vyapaar Dhanda - Data Compliance & DPDP Standards

Compliance guidelines under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act & IT Guidelines:

1. Complete User Data Sovereignty

Shopkeepers retain 100% full ownership over all customer credit records, supplier accounts, employee staff payrolls, and stock data stored within the app. Data is never shared with third parties.

2. Customer & Supplier Consent Notice

Shopkeepers recording customer names, phone numbers, and transactional credit details (Udhar Khata) must ensure appropriate consent from their respective customers in accordance with local regulations.

3. Right to Data Rectification & Deletion

You can purge, edit, reset, or delete any stock item, invoice bill, customer profile, supplier record, or staff entry at any time using the built-in management tools in the app.

4. Storage Security & Android Sandbox Isolation

Local SQLite database storage operates strictly within standard Android sandbox isolation rules, ensuring that other application packages on the smartphone cannot access your business records.
